# Ge'ez Identity: Historical Roots, Cultural Significance, and Current Status

## Abstract
This research document explores the foundational role of the ancient **Ge'ez language** in Ethiopian spiritual, cultural, and linguistic identity. The study analyzes its historical origins, the mystical and mathematical significance of its characters and numerals, its role within the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, and its status in modern Ethiopia. Furthermore, it addresses the challenges of preserving the language for future generations and suggests potential solutions.

## Objectives
* **Examine Historical Significance**: Demonstrate the fundamental role of Ge'ez in Ethiopian history, culture, and religion.
* **Reveal Numerical Mysteries**: Explain the hidden mathematical (Qemer) and symbolic meanings within Ge'ez letters and numbers.
* **Assess Current Awareness**: Evaluate the general public's knowledge of the language through research.
* **Modernize the Language**: Identify strategies to integrate Ge'ez into the modern education system and technology.

## Structure
The study is organized into five main chapters:
1. **Chapter 1 - Introduction**: Background of the study, problem statement, and objectives.
2. **Chapter 2 - Literature Review**: Analysis of prior scholarly works on Ge'ez.
3. **Chapter 4 - Methodology**: A mixed research approach utilizing surveys and interviews.
4. **Chapter 4 - Data Analysis**: Analysis of data collected from 50 participants and interpretation of Ge'ez characters.
5. **Chapter 5 - Conclusion and Recommendations**: Key findings and suggestions for future action.

## Key Findings
* **Identity Marker**: Ge'ez serves as the bedrock of Ethiopia's "Golden Age of Civilization."
* **Knowledge Gap**: While many young people accept the language as a cultural foundation, the study confirms that practical proficiency and understanding remain low.
* **Necessity of Technology**: The development of digital tools and mobile applications is identified as a critical factor in preserving the language.

## Methodology
The research primarily utilized the following methods:
* Questionnaires collected from 50 diverse participants via **Google Forms**.
* Interviews with language experts and scholars.
* Analysis of historical documents and parchment manuscripts (Birana).

---
> **Note:** This study emphasizes Ge'ez not only as a liturgical language but also as a national and global source of indigenous knowledge.
